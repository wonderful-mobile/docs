## Thanks

Wonderful Kernel builds on the work of many open-source projects, including
[KernelSU-Next](https://github.com/KernelSU-Next/KernelSU-Next),
[SukiSU](https://github.com/SukiSU-Nova/SukiSU-Nova),
[BakaSU](https://github.com/Baka-SU/BakaSU),
[SUSFS](https://gitlab.com/simonpunk/susfs4ksu) by simonpunk,
[sidex15](https://github.com/sidex15/KernelSU-Next)'s KernelSU-Next SUSFS branch,
[JackA1ltman](https://github.com/JackA1ltman/NonGKI_Kernel_Build_2nd)'s SUSFS port for non-GKI kernels,
[AnyKernel3](https://github.com/osm0sis/AnyKernel3) by osm0sis,
and [rsuntk](https://github.com/rsuntk), who started this kernel's build tooling.

Root manager logos belong to their projects. The BakaSU logo is by
[OukaroMF](https://github.com/OukaroMF), licensed
[CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/), and is
shown unmodified.
